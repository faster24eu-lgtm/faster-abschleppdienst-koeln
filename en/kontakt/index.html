When to call, when to use the form?

Call or WhatsApp: for a breakdown, an accident, or if you're stuck somewhere. A WhatsApp location is the fastest option.

Form: for planned transports, vehicle relocations, or questions that aren't urgent.

What to include

  • Where the vehicle is (location, street, and on a motorway the direction and exit)
  • Make, model and drivetrain
  • What happened
  • Where it should be taken

For a breakdown around Cologne or between Karlsruhe and Offenburg, you'll find more guidance on the Towing Service Cologne and Karlsruhe to Offenburg pages.
