5. Google Ads: conversion and call tracking

This website includes the Google tag from Google Ads (provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). We use it to measure whether a visit from a Google ad leads to a phone call: the phone number visible in the text is replaced in the browser with a Google number, through which the call still reaches us; we also anonymously record whether the phone number or the WhatsApp button was clicked. This processing only takes place if you have previously consented via the cookie banner; without your consent, tracking stays disabled. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6 para. 1 lit. a GDPR in conjunction with § 25 para. 1 TDDDG (German Telecommunications-Telemedia Data Protection Act). This may involve data being transferred to Google in the USA. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time, with future effect, via the "Cookie settings" link in this page's footer.

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in Belgium the Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit (Data Protection Authority).

We store personal data from your enquiry only for as long as necessary to process it, and delete it afterwards unless legal retention obligations require otherwise. Your cookie choice is stored exclusively in your own browser (localStorage), not on our servers.
