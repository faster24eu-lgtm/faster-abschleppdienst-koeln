Four boroughs, 51 localities

Bonn is divided into four boroughs, made up of 51 localities in total. Day to day, the well-known names matter: Beuel, Bad Godesberg, Hardtberg or Lengsdorf. Tell us these plus the street if you break down. The Rhine splits Bonn into a left-bank and a right-bank part, so tell us which side you're on too.

A565: very heavy traffic

The A565 crosses Bonn from north-east to south-west and is one of the most important routes for commuters and inner-city traffic. Between the Bonn-Lengsdorf exit and the Bonn-Nord interchange, it runs four lanes through densely populated area, in places with no hard shoulder in a cutting or on a bridge. This section carries over 100,000 vehicles a day and is prone to jams. Breaking down there means standing with no hard shoulder. Tell us this straight away on the phone so the driver can assess the situation.

A555: the Cologne-Bonn motorway

The A555 connects Cologne and Bonn and is known as the Cologne-Bonn motorway, one of the oldest motorway routes in Germany. More on this section at Breakdown on the A555.

A59 and A3: the right bank and the east

The A59 connects Cologne with Bonn-Beuel; at the Bonn-Ost interchange it continues as the right-bank Rheinuferstraße (B42), still built to motorway standard as far as Bad Honnef. To the east, the A3 crosses the A560 at the Bonn/Siegburg interchange, more on the A3 at Breakdown on the A3. If you break down on the right bank, tell us the motorway or federal road and your direction of travel.

Waiting safely in Bonn

On the A565, A555, A59 or A3: hazard lights on, everyone behind the crash barrier, set up a warning triangle if it's safe to do so. On the A565 with no hard shoulder: stay belted in the vehicle if possible until traffic is secured, and tell us right away that there's no hard shoulder. On a slope in Bad Godesberg: apply the handbrake and engage a gear, hazard lights on before you get out.

Where it often happens in Bonn

On the A565 between Bonn-Lengsdorf and the Bonn-Nord interchange, heavy use and the missing hard shoulder make it especially accident-prone. At the Bonn-Ost interchange, where the A59 becomes the B42, motorway and federal road switch abruptly. At the Bonn/Siegburg interchange, where the A3 and A560 meet, traffic is denser because of lane changes.

How to describe your location in Bonn

  • District and street, plus which bank of the Rhine (left or right).
  • On the A565, A555, A59 or A3: number, direction, exit or interchange.
  • Whether a hard shoulder is present or not.
  • On a slope in the Godesberg district: uphill or downhill.

Frequently Asked Questions About Bonn

Can you take my car from Bonn to my garage, even outside the city?
Yes, we take your vehicle to your own garage, a brand dealer workshop, or home, including outside Bonn. Tell us the destination and we'll arrange the trip with the partner.
Why is the A565 tricky?
The section between Lengsdorf and the Bonn-Nord interchange carries over 100,000 vehicles a day and in places has no hard shoulder. A breakdown there leaves you standing in moving traffic.
What is the Cologne-Bonn motorway?
The common name for the A555 between Cologne and Bonn, one of the oldest motorway routes in Germany.
I'm on the right bank. What do I say?
The motorway or federal road, direction, and exit, plus that you're on the right bank.
