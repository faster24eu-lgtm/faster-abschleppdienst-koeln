The roads around Karlsruhe

The Südtangente is a motorway-standard road: it starts at the Karlsruhe-Mitte exit and runs over the Maxau Rhine bridge to Wörth am Rhein in the Palatinate. Breaking down here often means standing in flowing traffic with no hard shoulder. Hazard lights, hi-vis vest, and if it's safe, behind the crash barrier. Then call us.

On the motorway and the Südtangente: don't stay seated in the vehicle and don't try to repair anything yourself. Your safety comes before the vehicle.

The tunnel under the Kriegsstraße

The B10 runs east to west through central Karlsruhe. Since mid-October 2022, through traffic no longer runs above ground along the Kriegsstraße but through two tunnel tubes, each around one and a half kilometres long, known as the Karoline-Luise-Tunnel. Above ground, the former up-to-ten-lane Kriegsstraße was rebuilt as a boulevard with a tram line.

If your vehicle stops in the tunnel, the same rules apply as in any road tunnel: hazard lights on, engine off, leave the vehicle and head to the nearest emergency point, if it's safe to do so. If anyone is injured, call 112. As soon as you're standing safely, call us. We'll arrange to get the vehicle out of the tunnel.

City centre and the tram network

Karlsruhe has a dense tram network. With the "Kombilösung" project, trams run through the city centre under the Kaiserstraße in a tunnel, but road traffic and trams still run close together at ground level in many places. If your vehicle is blocking tracks or a stop, please tell us straight away on the phone and also report it to the transit operator. Don't try to push the vehicle out of the way yourself if a tram is approaching.

Typical situations in Karlsruhe

  • Flat battery after the night: especially common in winter. We try jump-starting first before towing. More in our guide (German) Jump-starting and a flat battery.
  • Tyre damage on the B10 or B3: many new cars only carry a puncture repair kit instead of a spare wheel. If that's not enough, we take the vehicle to a tyre service.
  • Accident in the city: the vehicle is loaded and taken to the garage of your choice. Who covers the cost is explained in our guide (German) Towing costs after an accident.
  • Stuck in a car park or underground garage: tell us the clearance height. Not every tow truck fits everywhere, and we send the right vehicle.
  • EV out of charge: electric cars aren't simply towed, they're transported on a flatbed. See our guide (German) Towing an electric car.
  • Wrong fuel at the petrol station: don't start the engine, ignition off. See our guide (German) Wrong fuel.

Vans, tradespeople, company vehicles

If a loaded van has broken down, it matters to us how heavy it is and whether the load can stay in the vehicle. Vehicles up to 3.5 tonnes are our everyday business. For anything larger, tell us the length, height and weight, and we'll work out which vehicle is needed. What details count is in our guide (German) Towing a van.

What does it cost in Karlsruhe?

A price comparison site (kostenfinder.com, 2026) lists an average of around €219 for Karlsruhe, with a range of about €102 to €383. Our fixed price within the city starts at €129. We tell you the exact price for your route and vehicle in advance. More in our guide (German) Cost guide.

Are you an automobile club member?

Then call your automobile club or insurer first. If that takes too long, or you have no breakdown cover, we're here for you.

Frequently Asked Questions About Karlsruhe

Do you tow from the Südtangente or the B10?
Yes, tell us the last exit or the next junction. Where an authority or the police direct the response, the choice of company can sometimes be prescribed.
Can my car go to the garage of my choice?
Yes. After an accident that wasn't your fault, it should usually be the nearest suitable garage; more on that in our accident guide.
Do you also come to Ettlingen, Rheinstetten or Durlach?
Yes. These places are part of our Karlsruhe service area.
What do I do in a breakdown in the Kriegsstraße tunnel?
Hazard lights on, engine off, head to the nearest emergency point if it's safe to do so. If anyone is injured, call 112, then call us.
Can I just send my location?
Yes, on WhatsApp. On your phone you can share your current location there. That helps us find you quickly.
