Notable spots in and around Baden-Baden

The Michaelstunnel on the B500

The B500 runs as a western bypass past the city centre. The Michaelstunnel is about 2.5 kilometres long, built between 1986 and 1989, with a single tube and two-way traffic. If you break down there, switch on your hazard lights, turn off the engine, leave the vehicle and head to the nearest emergency point, if it's safe to do so. If anyone is injured, call 112. Call us as soon as you're standing safely. We'll arrange the rest.

The Schwarzwaldhochstraße

The B500 continues as the Schwarzwaldhochstraße for more than 60 kilometres to Freudenstadt, climbing above 1,100 metres. Steep sections put strain on brakes and cooling. In snow and ice, vehicles can slide or get stuck in winter; see our guide (German) Winter breakdown on the Schwarzwaldhochstraße. If you break down going up or down hill: hazard lights, hi-vis vest, warning triangle, and keep your distance from the carriageway. For pickup from mountain sections, please tell us the exact spot, for example a car park or viewpoint like the Mummelsee, Plättig, or Bühlerhöhe.

Narrow lanes and hillside streets

Baden-Baden's residential areas have narrow streets, gradients and bends. Briefly describe how your vehicle is positioned and whether it's blocking anything. We'll then send a vehicle that can manage there too.

Baden Airpark and the area around Rheinmünster

Karlsruhe/Baden-Baden Airport, known as Baden Airpark, is in Rheinmünster, about 30 kilometres south of Karlsruhe and roughly 10 kilometres from Baden-Baden, Bühl and Rastatt, right by the A5, B36 and B500. From the A5 you can reach it via the Baden-Baden exit towards Iffezheim, or the Bühl exit (52) towards Oberbruch and Rheinmünster. If you're stuck in the car park with a flat battery after a trip, you usually just need a jump-start, not a tow truck. Tell us the car park and row, and we'll find you.

Towing to Bühl, Rastatt and Sinzheim

The distances between these towns are short, and your regular garage is often not far. We take your vehicle there, to a dealer, or home. For accidents: choose the destination carefully. The other driver's liability insurance usually covers the trip to the nearest suitable garage. See our guide (German) Towing costs after an accident.

Motorcycles in the mountains

The mountain roads are popular with motorcyclists. In a breakdown or after a fall, we transport motorcycles on a suitable vehicle, since motorcycles can't be towed. Tell us the model and whether it still rolls. More in our guide (German) Motorcycle transport after a breakdown or fall.

Frequently Asked Questions About Baden-Baden

Do you come as far as the Schwarzwaldhochstraße?
Yes, that's possible. Because of the distance and the mountain road, we quote the price individually on the phone.
What do I do in a breakdown in the Michaelstunnel?
Hazard lights on, engine off, get out and head to the nearest emergency point, if it's safe to do so. If anyone is injured, call 112. Then call us.
Do you help in Rastatt and Bühl too?
Yes. Rastatt, Sinzheim, Bühl, Gaggenau and Gernsbach are part of our service area.
I'm at Baden Airpark. Can you help?
Yes. Tell us the car park and vehicle. Often a jump-start is enough.
Can I get a rental car?
We don't offer this ourselves. If needed, ask your insurer or automobile club whether a replacement vehicle is included.
