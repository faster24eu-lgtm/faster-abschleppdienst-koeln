Between Appenweier and Offenburg is the Gottswald/Waldmatten rest area, and the motorway crosses the Kinzig on a bridge. At Offenburg, the six-lane section of the A5 ends, and the motorway continues south with only four lanes. Where the carriageway narrows, traffic jams are more frequent. If you break down in a jam, switch on your hazard lights and keep the emergency lane clear. South of Offenburg follow the Höfen/Korb, Unditz/Schutter and Kürzell/Gerstenmatt rest areas, then the Mahlberg service station.

Kehl and Strasbourg

At Appenweier the B28 branches off towards the Rhine border at Kehl. The Europe Bridge between Kehl and Strasbourg is a four-lane road bridge from 1960 and is seen as a symbol of Franco-German reconciliation. Many vehicles come from France, others are heading there. We speak German, French, Dutch and English. If your vehicle needs to cross the border after a breakdown, or if you're registered in France and break down in the Ortenau, ask us about the options and the price. Cross-border transports are always discussed in advance on the phone. What's different in France, for example on motorways and in Strasbourg's low-emission zone, is covered in our guide (German) Breakdown near Kehl and Strasbourg.

Kinzig valley and the Black Forest

Offenburg is the starting point into the Kinzig valley towards Gengenbach and further into the Black Forest. On mountain roads, brakes and cooling matter. If your vehicle gives out there, send us your location on WhatsApp, ideally with a photo of the vehicle and its surroundings.

Lahr, Ettenheim, Rust

South of Offenburg, many travellers leave the A5 at Lahr (B415, with Lahr airport) or at Rust, where the Europa-Park theme park has its own exit. After a long day at the theme park, it's common for the battery to be flat because the lights were left on. Tell us the car park and the vehicle. In many cases the problem can be fixed on the spot.

An important junction

Offenburg is not just a motorway exit but also a traffic junction: routes into the Kinzig valley and the Black Forest split here, the B28 leads towards Kehl and Strasbourg, and the railway also crosses the Rhine valley here. For a breakdown, that means: the route to the right garage can be short, or it can cover the whole stretch. Tell us your destination and we'll work out how to get there.

If the transport crosses the border

For trips to France, have your ID and vehicle documents ready, along with the licence plate and vehicle type. For vehicles without registration or with export plates, the process is different from a normally registered car. Please tell us this in advance.

After towing: what happens next?

Once your vehicle is at the garage, ask about the diagnosis and repair time there. Ask for a cost estimate before repairs begin, and about a replacement vehicle if your insurance or breakdown cover provides one. Keep the invoice and receipts for the towing costs.
