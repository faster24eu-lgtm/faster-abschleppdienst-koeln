What happens after you call?

You tell us briefly what happened and give us your location, fastest by WhatsApp. We check with you what's needed and tell you the price before anyone drives out. After that our partner on the ground takes over, and you stay in touch with us until everything's sorted.

How we work

Faster is based in Antwerp, Belgium. In Germany we work with partners who carry out the job on site, for example in Cologne, Karlsruhe, Baden-Baden, Achern and Offenburg, but also in many other cities from Hamburg to Munich. You can reach us around the clock, we coordinate the help and stay your point of contact on the phone.

If you have time, have a look at our guides (currently in German): Ratgeber explain what to do after a breakdown, what matters when towing a vehicle, and who pays after an accident.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you get there?
That depends on your location, traffic and availability. We give you a realistic estimate on the call rather than a fixed promise.
What does the help cost?
That depends on location, destination, vehicle and situation, so it can't be quoted flat on a website. Call or message us and we'll tell you the price on the phone before anyone drives out.
Are you reachable at night and on weekends?
Yes, by phone and WhatsApp we're reachable around the clock, including nights, weekends and public holidays.
Do you help outside Cologne and Karlsruhe too?
Yes, we coordinate partners nationwide. Tell us your location and we'll work out how we can help.
Where is Faster based, and who actually comes to me?
Faster is based in Antwerp, Belgium. The jobs on the ground are carried out by partners; we coordinate the help and stay your point of contact on the phone.
