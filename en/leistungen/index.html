Roadside help on the spot

Many breakdowns can be fixed at the roadside, so your car does not need towing at all. Typical cases:

  • Flat battery: a jump start on the spot is often enough.
  • Flat tyre: a wheel change if you have a spare, otherwise transport to the nearest tyre shop.
  • Wrong fuel: do not start the engine. Depending on the case the tank is pumped out or the car goes to a workshop.
  • Car won’t start or locked out: tell us what happens and we work out what is needed.

Towing and recovery

If the car can no longer be driven, a partner takes it where you want it: your workshop, a dealer or home. You hear the price on the phone before anyone drives out. Short jobs often start at €125. What affects the price is explained in our cost guide.

After an accident other rules often apply, for example who pays. More here: towing costs after an accident. Broken down on the motorway? First read what to do right away.

Vehicles that need extra care

Not every vehicle can simply be lifted onto a hook. Tell us what you drive when you call:

  • Electric cars and automatics: usually on a flatbed, not with one axle lifted.
  • Vans, motorhomes or car and trailer: these need the right recovery vehicle.
  • Motorcycles: transported and strapped down, not towed.
  • Underground or multi-storey car parks: height and access decide which vehicle can come.
