Towing in Cologne: What Does the Cost Depend On?

A single price for every case would be misleading. Here's what determines the effort involved in Cologne, and what to ask before you commit.

We deliberately don't quote prices on this page. We discuss what a callout costs with you personally, once we know the situation.

The factors at a glance

FactorWhy it changes the effort
Location and destinationThe distance between your location, the garage, and the vehicle determines time and mileage.
Side of the RhineOnly a few bridges connect the left and right banks, which can lengthen the route.
Motorway or cityDifferent safety requirements apply on the ring road, and there can be traffic jams.
Time of daySurcharges are common at night, on weekends, and on public holidays.
Vehicle typeCars, vans, motorcycles or electric cars each need different equipment.
ConditionA rolling vehicle is easier to load than one that's locked up or an accident wreck.
LocationAn underground garage, a narrow street, or a building site needs the right vehicle and more time.

What's special about Cologne

Cologne lies on both banks of the Rhine, and the ring formed by the A1, A3 and A4 is among the most heavily used motorway connections in the country. A callout can therefore take longer than the distance suggests. If your vehicle is in an underground garage or car park, the clearance height decides which vehicle can come, see Underground garage and car park. For describing your location, the district pages help, for example Cologne-Ehrenfeld or Cologne-Porz.

Questions to ask before you commit

  1. Is this a fixed price or an estimate?
  2. Does it include the callout, loading, and all mileage?
  3. Is there a surcharge for nights, weekends or public holidays?
  4. Is VAT included?
  5. How can I pay?
  6. Exactly where will the vehicle be taken?

Get the answers in writing where possible, for example over WhatsApp.

Time is its own factor

In Cologne, a callout on the motorway ring can take longer than the distance suggests because of traffic. That affects the cost, since waiting time and driving time are both part of what you pay for. The same applies to callouts in the city centre during roadworks or closures. So ask whether waiting time is included in the price, and tell us if your situation is urgent: sometimes the choice of Rhine bridge decides the travel time.

How it works with us

  1. You call or message us on WhatsApp and send your location.
  2. We clarify the vehicle, its condition, the destination and the time.
  3. We discuss the cost with you personally, before anyone drives out.
  4. The partner on the ground carries out the job; we stay your point of contact.

When someone else pays

After an accident that wasn't your fault, the other driver's liability insurance usually covers the towing costs, see Towing costs after an accident. Breakdown cover or club membership can also cover the help. Check your documents before you pay. General reference prices for towing services in Germany are in our general cost guide.

Who pays, who drives?

Faster is based in Antwerp, Belgium. In and around Cologne, we work with partners who carry out the job on the ground. We coordinate the help and discuss the cost with you before anything starts.
