Breakdown at the Leverkusen Bridge

The A1 crosses the Rhine between Leverkusen and Köln-Niehl on a cable-stayed bridge over a kilometre long. Elevated sections and interchanges lie before and after it: a spot where a breakdown blocks traffic fast.

Between the Leverkusen interchange and Köln-Niehl, then, is a sequence of elevated sections and bridges together about 2.5 kilometres long, with few places to pull over. After the bridge, the route passes Merkenich and a large industrial park in Cologne before reaching the Cologne-Nord interchange near Chorweiler.

A structure under renewal

Because of significant structural damage, the Leverkusen Rhine bridge is being replaced with a new bridge, one per direction. The first ground was broken on 14 December 2017, and the regional roads authority (Landesbetrieb Straßenbau NRW) planned the new bridge with ten lanes. While the damage persisted, the old bridge was at times restricted to vehicles under 3.5 tonnes, which diverted traffic onto other Rhine bridges. The current status, which lanes are open, and whether restrictions apply can change. Check a traffic report before you drive.

What applies in a breakdown on a bridge or elevated section

  • React early: as soon as your vehicle loses power, indicate and move right, while you can still steer.
  • Avoid stopping in the middle of the bridge if you can help it. Coast past the bridge or to the next exit.
  • Hazard lights, hi-vis vest, behind the crash barrier, once you've reached the edge. If anyone is injured, call 112.
  • If you can't reach the edge: stay belted in and call 112. Getting out in the middle of the carriageway is life-threatening.

The detailed steps are in our guide (German) Breaking down on the motorway. For the Cologne side, see Breakdown on the A1 near Cologne, and for the district, Towing Service Cologne-Nippes.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Leverkusen Bridge

How long is the Leverkusen Rhine bridge?
It's 1,057 metres long and a cable-stayed bridge. Before it lie elevated sections of 928 and 527 metres.
What's the first exit after the bridge?
Köln-Niehl (100), followed by the Cologne-Nord interchange (101).
Is the bridge closed?
That changes. Check current traffic reports; we don't know today's status.
What do I do if I break down on the bridge?
Hazard lights, move right if possible, hi-vis vest, and behind the crash barrier. If anyone is injured, call 112. Then call us and tell us the direction.
