Breakdown on the A57 Near Cologne

The A57 comes from the Lower Rhine and Neuss, crosses the A1 near Chorweiler, and ends in the middle of Köln-Ehrenfeld. Along this stretch, the number of lanes and the surroundings change several times, and that affects how you should behave in a breakdown.

A57 exits in the Cologne area

The A57 runs from the Dutch border near Goch, parallel to the Rhine via Krefeld and Neuss, to Cologne. It's part of European route E31, which runs from Rotterdam to Hockenheim. In Cologne, from north to south, you pass these points:

Where the motorway ends, in Ehrenfeld

After the Köln-Ehrenfeld exit, the A57 ends. From there the route continues as the K 4 to the Innere Kanalstraße and the B55a. If you break down in this area, you're suddenly no longer on a motorway but on a city street with traffic lights, junctions and pedestrians. It matters to us whether you're still on the motorway or already on the K 4. We'll then work out which rules apply; see also Towing Service Cologne-Ehrenfeld.

Lanes and traffic management

Between Dormagen and Köln-Worringen there's a traffic-management system. Past the Cologne-Nord interchange, the A57 has five lanes, and six from Longerich onward. Six lanes means the right-hand edge is far from the middle. If your vehicle loses power, move right early and roll to a stop rather than stopping in a middle lane. If you can't reach the edge, switch on your hazard lights, stay belted in, and call 112.

The Cologne-Nord interchange

At the Cologne-Nord interchange near Chorweiler, the A57 meets the A1 and the Cologne motorway ring. Interchanges have many entry and exit ramps, so in a breakdown there we always ask two things: which direction did you come from, and which direction were you heading? More on the surroundings: Breakdown on the A1 near Cologne and Towing Service Cologne-Chorweiler.

What to tell us on the phone

  1. Motorway (A57) and direction: towards Cologne or towards Neuss
  2. Last exit or rest area (Esch/Weiler)
  3. Whether you're on the hard shoulder, in a lane, or already past the end of the motorway
  4. Vehicle and drivetrain
  5. Whether anyone is injured

The first steps to take are covered in our guide (German) Breaking down on the motorway. Once you've reached the edge: hi-vis vest on, get out, and go behind the crash barrier. If the police attend or you've used an emergency phone, a contracted company is often assigned; you may still state your preference.

Frequently Asked Questions About the A57 Near Cologne

Where does the A57 end?
In Köln-Ehrenfeld, after exit 30. From there the route continues as the K 4 to the Innere Kanalstraße.
Which exits does the A57 have in Cologne?
Köln-Worringen (26), Köln-Chorweiler (27), the Cologne-Nord interchange (28), Köln-Longerich (28), Köln-Bickendorf (29) and Köln-Ehrenfeld (30).
Are there rest areas on the A57 in northern Cologne?
Yes, the Esch/Weiler rest area between Worringen and Chorweiler.
What if I break down past the end of the motorway?
Tell us that you're already on the K 4 or the Innere Kanalstraße. City rules apply there, not motorway rules.
