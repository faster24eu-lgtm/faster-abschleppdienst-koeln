What belongs to the Nippes district

District 5 lies on the left bank of the Rhine north of central Cologne and covers the neighbourhoods Bilderstöckchen, Longerich, Mauenheim, Niehl, Nippes, Riehl and Weidenpesch. It was formed on 1 January 1975 as part of the municipal reorganisation and is named after its most populous neighbourhood. To the south it borders Neustadt-Nord, to the west Neuehrenfeld, and to the east lies the Rhine.

Neusser Straße and the neighbourhood

At the centre of the Nippes neighbourhood is Neusser Straße with many shops. Around it are two areas with their own history: the Eisenbahn-Quartier, also called the Sechzigviertel, which grew out of a former railway repair works' housing estate and is now partly car-free, and the Clouth Quartier on the site of the former Clouth rubber works, where a new residential area has been developing since 2013. Both areas have narrow streets, traffic-calmed zones and lots of parked cars. A tow truck needs room to manoeuvre there, so tell us how tight it is at your spot.

Niehl, Riehl and the Rhine

Niehl lies in the north on the Rhine, and Riehl is home to Cologne Zoo. Niehler Straße, formerly the Niehler Weg, is considered the oldest street in Nippes and was likely already a Roman through-route. The Köln-Niehl exit (100) of the A1 gives direct motorway access, beyond which lies the Cologne-Nord interchange, where the A1 and A57 meet. If you break down there, tell us the direction and whether you're before or past the interchange. More information: Breakdown on the A1 near Cologne and Breakdown on the A57.

Tram line to the north

Tram line 15 runs from Ubierring through Nippes, Weidenpesch and Longerich to Chorweiler. On the approach roads to these stops, cars are often parked in a second row. If your vehicle is blocking a stop or a tram lane, please tell us right away and also inform the transit operator.

Longerich and Weidenpesch: close to the motorway in the north

In the north of the district lie Longerich and Weidenpesch. The Köln-Longerich exit (28) of the A57 gives motorway access, and tram line 15 connects both neighbourhoods with Nippes and Chorweiler. In a breakdown there, the exit is the best landmark: tell us whether you're before or past it, and whether you're heading towards the city centre or towards the Cologne-Nord interchange.

How to describe your location in Nippes

  • Name the neighbourhood: Nippes, Niehl, Riehl, Longerich, Mauenheim, Weidenpesch or Bilderstöckchen.
  • On Neusser Straße, the cross street or house number.
  • In the Sechzigviertel or Clouth Quartier: the street and whether you're in a traffic-calmed zone.
  • On the motorway: the Köln-Niehl exit (100) or the Cologne-Nord interchange, direction and which side of the carriageway.

Frequently Asked Questions About Nippes

Which neighbourhoods belong to the Nippes district?
Bilderstöckchen, Longerich, Mauenheim, Niehl, Nippes, Riehl and Weidenpesch.
I'm at the Cologne-Nord interchange. What do I say?
The motorway and direction, whether you're on the A1 or A57, and whether you're before or past the interchange. Best of all, send your location on WhatsApp.
Do you come into the Clouth Quartier or the Sechzigviertel?
That depends on access. Tell us the street and how tight it is, and we'll work out which vehicle fits.
Is Riehl still part of Nippes?
Yes, Riehl is one of the neighbourhoods of the Nippes district.
