No ticket system, no waiting on hold: you speak to us right away.

What happens after you call?

You tell us briefly what happened and send us your location, easiest by WhatsApp. We work out with you the vehicle and destination and tell you the price before anyone drives out. After that our partner on the ground picks up your vehicle and brings it to the agreed place.

Towing after a breakdown or accident

From a classic tow to an accident, fire or serious damage: we collect your vehicle safely and bring it to a garage, home, or another destination. The vehicle is usually loaded onto a flatbed. That protects the gearbox, axles and electronics, and even vehicles that can no longer roll or steer can still be transported.

Recovery: when the vehicle isn't on the road

If a vehicle is in a ditch, on an embankment, or wedged between obstacles, it's not just towing that's needed but recovery. Tell us how the vehicle is positioned, whether it's tipped over, and whether fuel or oil is leaking. If anyone is injured or there's a fire risk, call 112 first. Our guide (German) on whether a car is still driveable after an accident can also help.

How to prepare

  • Have the vehicle registration and keys ready
  • Take personal belongings and valuables with you
  • After an accident, take photos of the vehicle and the scene
  • Give us the garage or destination address
  • For electric or hybrid vehicles, mention it right away on the phone

Where can the vehicle go?

In principle, wherever you want it: your usual garage, the brand's dealership, a dealer, or your home. After an accident someone else caused, their liability insurer usually only covers the trip to the nearest suitable garage. How that works exactly is explained in our guide (German) on towing costs after an accident.

Vehicle transport without urgency

Not every trip is urgent. If the vehicle is already parked safely, we can plan the transport for a convenient time, for example to a garage, a dealer, or a buyer or seller. That also applies to unregistered vehicles, accident-damaged cars and classic cars, where the transport needs to be especially gentle.

Vehicles

  • Cars and vans up to 3.5 tonnes
  • Motorcycles and scooters (guide, German)
  • Electric and hybrid vehicles (guide, German)
  • Machinery and forklifts

Towing on motorways around Cologne

Things work a little differently on the motorway: a vehicle is taken off at the next exit, and if the police are involved, a contracted company is often assigned. More on our Breakdown on the Cologne Motorway Ring page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my vehicle pulled or carried?
Usually carried: it's loaded onto a flatbed. That's gentler, and for electric cars and motorcycles it's the right choice.
Can I ride along in the tow truck?
We work that out case by case. Tell us how many people there are.
What if I don't have the key?
Tell us on the phone. A vehicle without a key is often harder to load, but we'll find a solution.
Do you also come from an underground car park?
If the clearance height and space allow it, yes. Tell us the height and access route, and we'll send the right vehicle.
