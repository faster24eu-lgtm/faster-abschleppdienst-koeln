Breakdown on the A59 Near Cologne

The A59 between Cologne and Bonn is the right-bank link that serves the airport. It starts at the Cologne-Heumar triangle and runs through south-eastern Cologne to Bonn-Beuel.

Three separate A59 sections

The A59 exists as three unconnected sections. The northernmost is in Duisburg, another runs from Düsseldorf to Leverkusen, and finally the A59 connects Cologne with Bonn-Beuel. This third section is what this page covers. It starts at the Cologne-Heumar triangle, where the A3 and A4 converge.

Between the Porz triangle and the Airport exit, the A59 has the Schloss Röttgen petrol station. It's a useful landmark if you only know you're somewhere between two exits.

Cologne/Bonn Airport

Exit 34 leads to Cologne/Bonn Airport. Car parks P1 to P3 sit right by the terminals. For car park P2, parking portals list a maximum vehicle height of 1.95 metres; the sign at the entrance is what's reliable. If you return from holiday to a flat battery in the car park, you usually just need a jump-start, not transport. How to go about that is in our guide (German) Jump-starting and a flat battery. Height and ramps are covered in our guide (German) Underground garage and car park.

Many connections over a short distance

Between Heumar and Bonn-Ost, more than a dozen exits, triangles and interchanges follow one another. Many vehicles change lanes there. In a breakdown that means: don't just say "on the A59", tell us the last exit and the direction, towards Bonn or towards Cologne.

What to tell us on the phone

  1. A59 and direction, towards Bonn or Cologne
  2. Last exit or triangle
  3. Whether you're on the hard shoulder, in a lane, or at the airport
  4. Vehicle, drivetrain, and whether it still rolls
  5. Whether anyone is injured

The basic steps for a motorway breakdown are in our guide (German) Breaking down on the motorway. The district where most of the A59 runs within Cologne's city limits is Cologne-Porz.

After the trip: a typical situation at the airport

Many breakdowns at the airport are "standing-time" breakdowns: leave your vehicle in the car park for days or weeks, and you can come back to a flat battery, since batteries discharge even while parked. A jump-start is usually enough. For hybrid and electric vehicles, the connection points are set by the manufacturer; see our guide (German) Towing an electric car. If you're tired after a trip and your car won't start, tell us the car park and level, and save yourself the search.

Frequently Asked Questions About the A59 Near Cologne

Where does the A59 start near Cologne?
At the Cologne-Heumar triangle, where the A3 and A4 converge. The first exit is Rath (32).
How do I get to the airport?
Via the Airport exit (34) of the A59. Car parks P1 to P3 sit right by the terminals.
What is the Cologne-Porz interchange?
A motorway interchange (33) where the A559 joins. From there the A59 has five lanes.
Is there a landmark between Porz and the airport?
Yes, the Schloss Röttgen petrol station on the A59.
