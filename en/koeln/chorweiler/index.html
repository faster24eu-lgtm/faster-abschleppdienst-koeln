Towing Service Cologne-Chorweiler

Chorweiler is Cologne's northernmost district, and the only outer district that doesn't border the city centre. The A57 and A1 connect it to the rest of the city.

A district on the edge of the city

The district covers Blumenberg, Chorweiler, Esch/Auweiler, Fühlingen, Heimersdorf, Lindweiler, Merkenich (with Rheinkassel, Langel and Kasselberg), Pesch, Roggendorf/Thenhoven, Seeberg, Volkhoven/Weiler and Worringen. The Chorweiler neighbourhood itself lies about 14 kilometres north of the city centre. Forest and farmland surround the district to the north and west, and the Rhine meadows border it to the east. Because of this outlying position, distances to the city centre are long, and the connecting roads matter.

The "New Town"

The Chorweiler neighbourhood was built from 1966 onward as a large housing estate, the "Neue Stadt". It's considered the largest high-rise housing estate in North Rhine-Westphalia, with buildings up to 24 storeys. Traffic there is partly routed underground, with tunnel stations for the S-Bahn and tram. For you that means: breakdowns in the New Town often happen in car parks between high-rises and in underground garages. Tell us the street or house number and, for underground garages, the level and clearance height. Details at our guide (German) Underground garage and car park.

Fühlinger See and green spaces

To the east, parkland extends to the Fühlinger See recreation area, and to the south it merges, after crossing the motorway, into the outer green belt. On excursion days, car parks and access roads fill up. It matters to us which car park or entrance you're at.

The A57, A1 and Cologne-Nord interchange

The A57 has the Köln-Worringen (26) and Köln-Chorweiler (27) exits in the district, followed by the Cologne-Nord interchange (28) with the A1. In Merkenich, the route passes a large industrial park. On the A57 between Worringen and Chorweiler is the Esch/Weiler rest area as a landmark. If you break down, tell us the exit, direction (towards Cologne or Neuss), and whether you're before or past the interchange. More at Breakdown on the A57 and Breakdown on the A1 near Cologne.

S-Bahn and tram

Chorweiler is connected via S-Bahn line 11 and tram line 15, which terminates at Chorweiler station. A stranded vehicle still needs transport, so tell us the street and house number.

What we do here

We coordinate roadside assistance and towing in northern Cologne. Faster is based in Antwerp, Belgium; the jobs in Cologne are carried out by partners on the ground. For very long distances, we'll tell you on the phone what's realistic.

How to describe your location in Chorweiler

  • Name the neighbourhood: Chorweiler, Heimersdorf, Seeberg, Fühlingen, Merkenich, Worringen, and so on.
  • In the New Town: street, house number, or car park, and for underground garages the level.
  • On the A57: exit 26 or 27, towards Cologne or Neuss.
  • At the Fühlinger See: the car park or entrance.

Also in northern Cologne are Nippes and Ehrenfeld, connected to Chorweiler via the A57 and A1. All districts are listed at Cologne: Districts, and the Cologne homepage is Towing Service Cologne.

Frequently Asked Questions About Chorweiler

Does Chorweiler border the city centre?
No. Chorweiler is the only outer Cologne district that doesn't border the city-centre district.
Which A57 exits are in Chorweiler?
Köln-Worringen (26) and Köln-Chorweiler (27), followed by the Cologne-Nord interchange (28).
I'm in an underground garage in the New Town. What do I say?
The street, level, and clearance height from the sign. Not every tow truck fits into every underground garage.
Do you help in Worringen too?
Worringen is part of the district. We coordinate help across the Cologne area and work out on the phone how we can help with longer distances.
