<h2>Flat battery and won't start</h2>
<p>If the engine just clicks or does nothing at all, it's usually the battery. Common causes are cold weather, lots of short trips, an ageing battery, or a light left on. If it's safe and technically possible, we first try to start the vehicle with a booster. After that you should drive a longer stretch and have the battery checked. If the vehicle still won't start, there's often another fault, for example the starter motor or fuel supply. Then transport to a garage is the next step. Hybrid and electric cars have special jump-start points; see our guide (German) on <a href="ratgeber-starthilfe-batterie.html">jump-starts and flat batteries</a>.</p>
<h2>Flat Tyre</h2>
<p>Carrying on isn't always possible or safe. Many new vehicles don't have a spare wheel, only a repair kit with sealant. That helps with small holes in the tread, but not with a torn tyre or sidewall damage. In those cases, direct transport to a tyre service is often the most efficient solution. On motorways, in tunnels and at confusing spots, don't change a wheel yourself. More in our guide (German) on <a href="ratgeber-reifenpanne.html">flat tyres</a>.</p>
<h2>Locked Out</h2>
<p>Keys locked in the car, door shut? First check whether there's a spare key someone can bring, or whether your vehicle can be unlocked via a manufacturer app. If not, we'll see whether we can help you on the spot. Don't try to pry the door open with wire or a coat hanger: that often leads to scratches, bent door frames, damaged seals, or problems with the electronics and airbags. If a child or an animal is locked inside, call 112 immediately. More in our guide (German) on <a href="ratgeber-ausgesperrt.html">being locked out</a>.</p>
<h2>Wrong Fuel</h2>
<p>If you notice the mistake at the pump: stop fuelling, don't start the engine, and don't turn on the ignition. On many modern vehicles the fuel pre-supply pump starts running as soon as the driver's door opens, so keep the door closed if you can. The vehicle is then taken to a garage where the fuel is pumped out. If the engine has already run, the damage to the injection system can be significant. So the rule is: don't keep driving. More in our guide (German) on <a href="ratgeber-falsch-getankt.html">wrong fuel</a>.</p>
<h2>Vehicle Won't Start</h2>
<p>We first check what's going on, then decide whether help on the spot is enough or the vehicle needs towing. Your own observations help: Does it click? Does the starter motor turn over? Are warning lights on? Is the tank empty?</p>
<h2>Empty Tank</h2>
<p>If a vehicle runs out of fuel, we'll work out with you whether fuel can be brought to you or the vehicle taken to the nearest petrol station. On diesel vehicles, the fuel system often needs bleeding after running dry; the garage decides that.</p>

<h2>The Rhine bridge near Leverkusen</h2>
<p>The A1 crosses the Rhine between the Leverkusen-West interchange and Köln-Niehl on the Leverkusen bridge, a cable-stayed bridge just over a kilometre long. Together with its approach viaducts, the bridge is one of the network's bottlenecks. Due to significant structural damage it's being replaced by a new bridge, one per direction; the old bridge was at times restricted to vehicles under 3.5 tonnes. Whether anything is currently closed or restricted can change, so check a traffic report before you drive. If you break down here, tell us whether you're before or past the bridge.</p>
<h2>The noise-protection tunnel near Lövenich</h2>
<p>Near Köln-Lövenich, the A1 runs for about 1.5 kilometres in a noise-protection enclosure, because housing extends right up to the motorway. That counts as a tunnel. If you break down there, behave as in any tunnel: hazard lights on, switch off the engine, leave the vehicle and head to the nearest emergency point, if it's safe to do so. If anyone is injured, call 112. Right after the tunnel comes the Cologne-West interchange, where the A1 leaves the Cologne ring.</p>
<h2>The Cologne-Nord interchange near Chorweiler</h2>
<p>The Cologne-Nord interchange is near Chorweiler, where the A1 meets the A57. An interchange brings many traffic movements together, so in a breakdown it helps us to know which direction you came from and where you were heading. <h2>South of Cologne-West: four lanes, a truck stop and a rest area</h2>
