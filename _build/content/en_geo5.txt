<h2>The Münsterland and East Westphalia: the Bielefeld hill</h2>
<p>Passing south and east of Hamm, it crosses the Datteln-Hamm canal in the Uentrop district and the Lippe river, then runs through the northern part of the Beckum hills in the flat Münsterland. Past Rheda-Wiedenbrück and Gütersloh, it reaches East Westphalia. After crossing the A33 at the Bielefeld interchange follows the crossing of the Teutoburg Forest, also called the "Bielefelder Berg". On the north-eastern side of the ridge, the connection to Bielefeld's city centre follows. Near Herford it reaches the Werre valley, then climbs onto the Lipper Bergland hills. At the Bad Oeynhausen interchange, the A30 ends. More on this section: <a href="en-stadt-bielefeld.html">Towing Service Bielefeld</a>.</p>
<h2>The Weser and the Weser hills</h2>
<p>Near Bad Oeynhausen, the A2 crosses the A30, the western Weser floodplain, and the river itself on two separate bridge structures set some distance apart. On the eastern bank, the B482 connects, leading to Porta Westfalica and Minden. The crossing of the Weser hills follows; just before the summit at the Papenbrink, the state border switches from North Rhine-Westphalia to Lower Saxony. Near Bad Nenndorf it skirts the western end of the Deister hills, after which the North German Plain begins.</p>
<h2>Hannover, Braunschweig, Helmstedt</h2>
<p>Near Wunstorf it crosses the Mittelland canal and the Leine river. Near the Nordhafen, the A352 branches off; in the Altwarmbüchener Moor follow, in quick succession, the Hannover-Buchholz interchange with the A37 and the Hannover-Ost interchange with the A7, more at <a href="en-stadt-hannover.html">Towing Service Hanover</a> and <a href="en-autobahn-a7.html">Breakdown on the A7</a>. Then come Lehrte, the Hämeler Wald and Peine, followed by the A391 at the Braunschweig-Nord interchange and Braunschweig-Wolfsburg airport right north of the motorway. At the Wolfsburg/Königslutter interchange it crosses the A39. Near Helmstedt lies the former most important border crossing between the German states, where the transit route to West Berlin began. Some of the old processing facilities survive or have been converted into a service station.</p>
<h2>The Magdeburg plain, the Elbe, Brandenburg</h2>
<p>In the Lappwald forest east of Helmstedt, the A2 crosses into Saxony-Anhalt, crosses the Aller river, and reaches the almost treeless Magdeburg plain. Near Irxleben there's a panoramic view of Magdeburg. At the Magdeburg interchange it crosses the A14, then the A2 runs through the northern districts, crosses the Rothensee link canal and the Elbe, with the Magdeburg waterway crossing lying just to the north. It then continues on many dead-straight stretches past Burg towards Brandenburg, along the north-western edge of the Fläming and the Baruth ice-age valley, past Brandenburg an der Havel, to the Werder triangle on the Berlin ring. On these long straights, wind is a factor, more in our guide (German) <a href="ratgeber-panne-autobahn.html">Breaking down on the motorway</a>.</p>
