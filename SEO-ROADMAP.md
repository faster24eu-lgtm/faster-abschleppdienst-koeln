# SEO-ROADMAP – Faster Abschleppdienst (Köln + Karlsruhe bis Offenburg)

Persistent working memory for the SEO project. Update after every work cycle. Newest cycle at the bottom.

## 0. Ground rules (from the owner)
- No invented prices, response times, addresses, reviews, staff, vehicles, statistics or guarantees.
- **No prices for Köln.** The only price on the site is "Festpreis ab 129 €" for the region Karlsruhe–Offenburg (owner suggestion, still to be confirmed with the partners).
- Köln jobs are done by partners; Faster sits in Antwerpen (Belgium). This is disclosed on the site and must stay so.
- Formal "Sie". No thin city pages; every location page needs its own roads, situations and facts.
- Whole site is in **preview mode**: `noindex, nofollow` on every page and `robots.txt` = `Disallow: /`.
